# Application identity matrix

This matrix separates stable family traits from provisional product expression.
It does not assign final branding to applications that have not been designed.

## Shared family contract

All AI Bharatiyaa products share:

- Anek display typography and readable Noto/system body typography
- neutral surfaces with indigo for suite-level actions and focus
- semantic status colors with stable meanings
- 4px-based spacing, restrained radius, and explicit structure
- direct language and transparent AI operation states
- the same accessibility and responsive quality floor

## Accent rules

- The suite indigo remains the default until a product needs distinction.
- An application accent must have an accessible foreground pair.
- Accent never replaces success, warning, danger, or informational meaning.
- One dominant accent is enough for an application shell.
- Test accent use in actions, focus-adjacent surfaces, selection, and charts
  before approving it.

The living preview shows two deliberately provisional themes to demonstrate the
boundary: a shared structure can feel different without redefining its grammar.

## Naming and suite navigation

- Use `Product name · AI Bharatiyaa` for document titles where appropriate.
- Show the product name more prominently than the suite name inside a working
  application.
- A future suite switcher should identify the current product and preserve a
  predictable destination for returning to the portfolio.
- Do not add a suite switcher until at least two products are publicly available.
